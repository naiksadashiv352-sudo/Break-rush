A quick snowy race

Roll the dice, aim a snowball, and race to space 24.

How to play
  1. Roll a dice for 1–6 spaces.
  2. Launch when the arrow reaches the green zone. A hit adds 2 spaces. You have 5 seconds.
  3. Land on a friend to bump them back 2 spaces. Passing them is fine.
  4. Reach or cross space 24 to win. The starter rotates on rematches.

Friends share one device. Tap buttons, or focus them and press Enter or Space. Your snowball marker grows as you move!
